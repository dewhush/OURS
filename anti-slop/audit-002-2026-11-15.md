## HIGH Priority (Hard Gates & Critical Functional / Data / Security Bugs)

14. **[HIGH] [Data Loss / SQLite Queue Schema] `OursDatabase.location_queue` drops `activity`, `altitude_m`, and `vertical_speed_mps` telemetry on every queued location upload**
    - **Location**: `android/app/src/main/java/app/ours/data/local/OursDatabase.kt:39-50`, `OursDatabase.kt:103-141`, `android/app/src/main/java/app/ours/location/LocationTrackingService.kt:247-268`
    - **Reason**: `LocationTrackingService` computes motion `activity` (`still`, `walking`, `running`, `cycling`, `driving`, `stairs`), `altitudeM`, and `verticalSpeedMps` via `MotionTelemetryPipeline`, attaches them to `LocationPointDto`, and enqueues the point into `OursDatabase.enqueueLocation(point)`. Because the `location_queue` SQLite table only stores `(lat, lon, accuracy_m, speed_mps, bearing, battery_pct, charging, recorded_at)`, `peekLocations()` reconstructs every `LocationPointDto` with `activity = null`, `altitudeM = null`, and `verticalSpeedMps = null`, silently stripping all motion and barometric telemetry before upload.
