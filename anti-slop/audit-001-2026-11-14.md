## Numbered Findings

1. **[HIGH] [R-25 — Color Contrast] Light & Dark palette contrast failures (`Tokens.kt:43-95`, Issue #35)**
   - **Reason:** `OursLightColors.action` (`#D4623F` on `#FAF8F4` = `3.46:1`), `OursLightColors.inkMuted` (`#787570` on `#FAF8F4` = `4.29:1`, on `#F2EFEA` = `3.95:1`), `OursLightColors.success` (`#228B54` on `#E8F5EE` = `3.97:1`), and `OursDarkColors.inkMuted` (`#8A8882` on `#232320` = `4.38:1`) fail the WCAG AA `4.5:1` minimum for normal text and over translucent glass surfaces.

2. **[HIGH] [R-03 — Mobile Responsiveness] Map view clipped above floating bottom dock (`MainActivity.kt:380-385`, `MemoriesScreen.kt:80-155`, Issue #48)**
   - **Reason:** `Box(Modifier.fillMaxSize().padding(innerPadding))` applies bottom scaffold inset (`276px`) to the root container, cutting off the map at `y = 2064` and leaving a flat beige strip behind `OursFloatingBottomNav` instead of letting the map extend edge-to-edge under the glass dock.

3. **[HIGH] [R-03 — Mobile Responsiveness] Truncated CTA and header text on mobile width (`MapScreen.kt:357-388`, `SettingsScreen.kt:163-236,635-666`, Issues #50 & #51)**
   - **Reason:** Partner bottom sheet truncates `"Send thinking-of-you"` to `"Send thinking-of-..."` in the 2-column button row, and the Settings Couple Header Card duplicates the `Paired` chip and truncates `"Marchanda & Dewa..."` and `"Paired with Dewantoro · marc..."`.

4. **[HIGH] [R-03 — Mobile Responsiveness] Sub-44dp tap targets in Chat and Memories (`ChatScreen.kt:420-430,689-725`, `MemoriesScreen.kt:345-357`, Issue #36)**
   - **Reason:** The reply banner close button (`16.dp` icon with `4.dp` padding = `24.dp`), message action pill (`6.dp` vertical padding = ~`28.dp`), and memory card highlight toggle (`7.dp` vertical padding = ~`30.dp`) are smaller than the `44.dp` (`48.dp` Material) minimum touch target.

5. **[HIGH] [R-02 — Copywriting] Em dash characters in UI strings and toast messages (`OursStrings.kt:194,305`, `OursViewModel.kt:522`)**
   - **Reason:** `OursStrings.kt` and `OursViewModel.kt` contain forbidden em dash characters (`"Your location sharing is paused — tap to resume"`, `"Berbagi lokasimu dijeda — ketuk untuk lanjut"`, `"Queued — will upload when online"`).

6. **[HIGH] [R-27 — UI States] Silent error state on MapScreen and ChatScreen when initial data is empty (`MapScreen.kt:139-151`, `ChatScreen.kt:247-263`, Issue #43)**
   - **Reason:** When the initial network fetch fails while local state is empty, `MapScreen` and `ChatScreen` render the normal first-run empty state (`"Waiting for first location..."` / `"Say hello"`) instead of a retryable error state explaining the failure.

7. **[HIGH] [R-26 — Interactive Elements] Map camera zooms to blank world map (`zoom ~ 0`) and distance label reads stale cache (`OursMapView.kt:251-315`, `MapScreen.kt:122-137,408-425`, Issues #47 & #49)**
   - **Reason:** Auto-fitting two coordinates across continents (`> 100 km`) zooms the camera out to the Atlantic Ocean (`zoom ~ 0`) where neither marker is visible, and `distanceText` ignores `state.myLocation` in favor of stale `LocationManager` cache.

8. **[HIGH] [R-39 — Accessible Semantics & Interaction Affordances] Connection test status uses neutral text color for both pass and fail (`OnboardingScreen.kt:552-570`, `SettingsScreen.kt:610-630`, Issue #53)**
   - **Reason:** Server health check results (`"Connected"` vs. `"Unreachable"`) both render in neutral `colors.live` with no semantic color or status icon differentiation.

9. **[MEDIUM] [R-10 — Glassmorphism & R-37 Owner Direction] Upgrade to iOS 26+ Liquid Glass surface system (`Components.kt:105-335`, `Tokens.kt:43-95`, all screens)**
   - **Reason:** Current `OursGlassCard` and `OursGlassPill` use flat alpha fills (`#E0FFFFFF`) with a uniform `1.dp` border rather than iOS 26+ Liquid Glass (multi-stop refractive surface gradient, top-left specular rim highlight border, inner luminance, real backdrop blur on Android 12+ where layered over live map/canvas, and crisp foreground contrast).
   - *Note on R-10:* Choose **9A** for **Full iOS 26+ Liquid Glass override** across top bars, bottom dock, sheets, cards, and pills (owner override per R-37), or **9B** for **R-10 Dose-Capped Liquid Glass** on floating chrome (top bar, bottom dock, map overlays, and sheets) with solid high-contrast content cards.

10. **[MEDIUM] [R-12 — Shadow] Arbitrary ambient shadows without specular depth (`Components.kt:118-170`, `ChatScreen.kt`, `MemoriesScreen.kt`, `SettingsScreen.kt`)**
    - **Reason:** Cards and pills use generic drop shadows (`elevation = 4.dp..10.dp`) without an elevation hierarchy or subtle dark ambient shadow paired with a specular top-edge highlight.

11. **[MEDIUM] [R-06 — Typography] Overuse of wide-tracked uppercase labels (`Tokens.kt:140-175`, `SettingsScreen.kt`, `OnboardingScreen.kt`, `MapScreen.kt`)**
    - **Reason:** Multiple small labels, badges, and section headers rely on uppercase + wide letter-spacing (`0.8.sp..1.2.sp`), which reads as the default AI technical template instead of warm iOS 26+ editorial hierarchy (clean title-case/sentence-case with weight contrast).

12. **[LOW] [R-11 — Border Radius] Flat radius usage across nested glass containers (`Tokens.kt:107-115`, `Components.kt`, `SettingsScreen.kt`, `MapScreen.kt`)**
    - **Reason:** Inner buttons and chips inside cards and bottom sheets do not consistently follow concentric nested curvature (`R_outer = R_inner + padding`) required for an authentic iOS 26+ Liquid Glass feel.

13. **[LOW] [R-20 — Visual Identity & R-31 Written Reason] Missing `DESIGN.md` codifying the iOS 26+ Liquid Glass + Warm Terracotta design system and dials (`DESIGN.md`)**
    - **Reason:** The repository lacks a `DESIGN.md` recording the Design Read, Liveliness Dials (`ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2`), concentric curvature tokens, Liquid Glass specular recipe, and one-line reasons required by `R-31` and `R-37`.
